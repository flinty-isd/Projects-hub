Before you publish

Every news post follows the same reading order: a clear title, a short summary that says who is affected, the main content under descriptive headings, any action required with its deadline, attachment links, and a named business contact.

  1. Create the post from the HPUK News post template (Other News: Other News post).
  2. Write the summary in the page Description. It is what readers see in news views, emails and Teams.
  3. Upload attachments to Notices and Attachments and link to them. Do not attach files to the page itself.
  4. Fill in the page details (Page details, top right):
    • Department and Category: required. Department is who owns the message (not who publishes it); Category is what it is about. Choose one of each. Use Category Health and Safety for anything that belongs in the Safety section. Put the location in Port and urgency in Urgent, not in Department or Category. If nothing fits, ask Communications to add a term; use General Information only as a last resort.
    • Strategy theme: optional. Choose which part of our strategy the story supports: Our People, Our Business, Our Environment or One TEAM. You can choose more than one, or none for routine notices.
    • Port: required. Choose the ports affected, or All ports. There is no blank option, so a notice is never sent everywhere by accident.
    • Expiry date: the last day the notice is current. It leaves the news views the next morning. Leave blank for news that does not expire.
    • Include in weekly roundup: on by default. Turn off for posts that should not go in Monday's port digest.
    • Urgent: only for notices that must reach people now. Only Chloe, Rachel and Toby can send urgent notices: when they publish the post, it goes straight out by email and Teams to every port chosen. If anyone else marks a post Urgent, nothing is sent and Communications is told. The alert reaches licensed staff only: staff without a Microsoft licence get urgent notices through their own area's route, so tell the area as well.
  5. If the post is only for a group (for example managers), set Audience. Audience-targeted posts are never emailed to whole ports; Communications is told so it can send to the right group.
  6. Select Publish (or Post news). There is no approval step: publishers are pre-approved, so the post is live as soon as you publish it. Check it carefully first, especially an urgent post, because the alert goes out straight away. If you scheduled it, it goes live at the scheduled time.

Urgent notices

An urgent alert goes out once per port and channel. Editing and republishing the post afterwards does not send it again.

To send a correction as a new alert: set Notification round up by one, set Urgent alert sent back to No, and republish. Only Communications should do this.

A sent alert is not a read receipt. Email and Teams delivery does not prove that a colleague has read a notice.

Expiry and withdrawal

Expiry only removes a post from the current news views. It does not delete the page or its attachments, and anyone with the link can still open it. To withdraw a notice that must not be read, unpublish it and contact Communications.

All news content is kept for 7 years, including deleted pages and files.

Help
