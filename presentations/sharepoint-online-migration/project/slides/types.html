The benefit register

Nineteen benefits, five kinds

TypeRefsHow we treat it
Cash-releasingB-01 – B-02Quantified with Finance. The only figures that count as savings.
Cost avoidanceB-03 – B-04Real money, but stated separately and never added to savings.
Risk and complianceB-05 – B-09Shown by the state of the control. Never turned into a pound figure.
EfficiencyB-10 – B-14Measured from a baseline. Never assumed time saved × headcount.
CapabilityB-15 – B-19Reported as adoption. A capability nobody uses is not a benefit.

Source: Benefits Realisation Plan BLD-PR-006 v1.0, sections 1.1 and 2 · 5

Each kind of benefit gets its own treatment. Only cash-releasing benefits go in a business case as savings. Cost avoidance is real but separate. Risk reduction is shown by control state. Efficiency is measured, not estimated: one invented time-saving figure would discredit the whole register. Capability is reported as adoption.
