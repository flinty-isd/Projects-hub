Cost avoidance (not savings)

The honest net position: subscription cost vs. farm run cost plus avoided remediation, not vs. zero (D-03).

Source: Benefits Realisation Plan BLD-PR-006 v1.0, sections 2.1 and 3 · 7

The cash case rests on B-01 and B-02 alone, and we cannot state a figure until Finance confirms the farm run cost. That is why the infrastructure cost baseline must be captured before the farm is switched off. B-03 and B-04 are cost avoidance; we recognise them at closure but we do not add them to a savings total. The subscription is a real new cost and we should say so.
