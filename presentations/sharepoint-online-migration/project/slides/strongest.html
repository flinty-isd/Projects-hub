Risk and compliance

Strongest benefits: security, not savings

B-05

Unsupported platform removed

Before: one farm, unsupported since 11/04/2023

Target: no 2013 workload left after decommission

B-06

HTTPS, modern auth and MFA

Before: 0% of intranet content behind modern authentication

Target: 100% at decommission

B-07

A real cyber incident register

Before: a phone number and a mailbox, no register

Target: 100% of reports logged within 60 seconds, with a timestamp, from 14/10

All three can be shown to an auditor. None of them is a pound figure.

Source: Benefits Realisation Plan BLD-PR-006 v1.0, sections 2.2 and 4.3 · 6

The plan's own assessment: B-05, B-06 and B-07 are the strongest benefits. All three are risk reduction and all are demonstrable. B-07 is the one that depends on IT and the Service Desk: if telephone reports are not entered into the form, the register hollows out and the benefit never evidences itself. B-08 covers retention, sensitivity labels and Restricted Content Discovery; B-09 covers named site owners and review cycles.
