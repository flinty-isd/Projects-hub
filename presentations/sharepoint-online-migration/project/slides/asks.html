What we need from IT

Six things that make the benefits real

01

Keep the baselines. Farm cost and content currency, before the farm is switched off.

02

Pull the ticket data. Service Desk volumes by category, 12 months to 31/08/2026.

03

Log every phoned-in incident in the form from 14/10, or the register hollows out.

04

Protect 14–16/10. Hypercare days only. No PFL HR prep pulled forward.

05

Name an owner for every live site by 31/01/2027. Unowned content is how the old site failed.

06

Measure, never estimate. One invented figure discredits the whole register.

Sources: BLD-PR-006 v1.0 sections 1.2 and 4.2 · BLD-PR-005 v1.1 · 15

These six asks come straight from the failure modes in the benefits plan and the residual plan. Items 1 and 2 are for Carl Sharman's team and Finance. Item 3 is for the Service Desk, briefed on 13/10. Item 4 is for everyone scheduling work in October. Item 5 is the long-term one: every capability benefit depends on someone maintaining the content, and the platform does not stop the old Cyber Security site's failure from recurring.
