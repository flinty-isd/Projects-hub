This is the single most time-critical action for IT. Most pre-migration baselines were never captured. Two can still be recovered from the farm itself: the infrastructure and support cost, and the last-modified distribution that shows how stale the 2013 estate was. Both disappear the moment the farm is decommissioned, and they are the evidence behind the strongest benefits. Where no baseline exists, we measure forward and say so; we never estimate backwards.
