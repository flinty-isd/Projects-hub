How to read the benefits

Delivering 220 sites is an output.
The benefit is what changes afterwards.

Almost none of it has happened yet. The benefits come after closure, delivered by BAU teams, and they only count if we measure them against a baseline.

This is the framing for everything that follows. The programme delivered sites; the organisation gets benefits only when behaviour and cost actually change. That happens after closure and mostly lands on IT and the Service Desk to evidence. So the rest of this section is about what we will measure and how, not about claiming success today.
