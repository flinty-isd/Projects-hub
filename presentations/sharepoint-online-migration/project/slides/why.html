Why 2013 had to go

Four problems the old farm could not fix

Out of support

SharePoint 2013 support ended on 11/04/2023. One unpatched farm was still carrying the intranet.

Not secure

The intranet was served over plain HTTP. The browser flagged the Cyber Security site “Not secure”.

Unusable on a phone

Classic pages did not reflow, which shut out most operational staff.

A migration every cycle

On-premises meant a forced migration every five to seven years. SharePoint Online updates in place.

Source: Benefits Realisation Plan BLD-PR-006 v1.0 · 3

The case for moving was mainly about risk. The platform had been out of support since April 2023. Content went over plain HTTP; the Cyber Security site was flagged Not secure, which is not a good look for that site of all sites. Classic pages did not work on phones, so most operational staff could not really use the intranet. And every on-premises version ends in another migration programme. SharePoint Online removes that cycle.
